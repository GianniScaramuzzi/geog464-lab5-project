02 / INTERPRET

How are buildings selected?

Each outline is roof geometry from Montréal's 2016 building dataset. A roof is included when at least half of its mapped area overlaps an economic-activity planning zone. Its distance is measured from the roof outline to the nearest mapped local greenspace. The map separates outlines within 100 metres, over 100 through 500 metres, and over 500 metres. Some adjacent roof surfaces may be grouped, so mapped features are not guaranteed to correspond one-to-one with buildings. They are not property boundaries, business locations, or evidence that a building is vacant. The source is from 2016, so later construction or changes may be missing.

03 / SCOPE

What this map can—and cannot—tell us

This map identifies mapped roof outlines located mostly within designated economic-activity zones and shows their distance to mapped greenspaces. The outlines may group adjacent roof surfaces and do not identify businesses, property lots, ownership, vacancy, public access, or suitability for rehabilitation. The source data dates to 2016, and the zone-overlap threshold is a screening rule. Verify current conditions and property boundaries with more recent authoritative data before making site-level decisions.
